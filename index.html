How to play
  • Flip two cards. A match scores and you keep the turn.
  • Matched pairs stay face-up on the table — use them as landmarks.
  • Chain matches for combo multipliers (x2, x3…). Each match also refills the timer.
  • Miss or timeout ends your turn. You start with one random power; earn more every 2 matches.
  • Block — tap a rival to freeze them. Scanner flashes two pairs. Shuffle remixes the unknown.
